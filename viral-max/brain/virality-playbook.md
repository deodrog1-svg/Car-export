# Virality Playbook (Viral Max's core brain)

> Edit freely. Add new laws to the bottom. Your own rules in `improve/adjustments.md` override this file.

## The 10 laws of 10M+ view videos

1. **The first second decides everything.** Movement plus a bold claim or a number on screen in frame 1.
   No logos, no "hi guys", no intro.
2. **Open a loop, close it late.** Ask a question in second 1 ("How much is this in Korea?") and answer
   it at the end. Retention is what makes the algorithm push a video.
3. **Price shock is the strongest emotion in car content.** "Korea price vs your country price" makes
   people comment, share, and argue.
4. **Comments are fuel.** Leave something to argue about or guess: wrong-looking prices, "which would
   you pick?", "guess the year". Answer comments with new videos (reply-to-comment videos).
5. **Faces beat logos.** One recurring person (the user) becomes the "Korea car guy". People follow
   people, not companies.
6. **Native sound.** Real sounds (engine start, auction beeps, port horns, Korean street noise) plus
   one trending audio at low volume. Captions always on, since about 80% watch muted.
7. **Short and looped.** 15–35 seconds. The last frame should flow into the first so viewers rewatch.
8. **Series beat one-offs.** "Part 1 / Part 2" or a fixed format ("Guess the price #27") builds
   habit and followers.
9. **Exotic-everyday.** What's normal in Korea (car auctions, spotless 3-year-old cars, port
   mountains of cars, Korean features like ventilated seats) is exotic abroad. Film Korea's
   everyday life.
10. **Every video sells softly.** CTA = one keyword: "Comment PRICE + your city". Then DM them.
    Make the call to action feel like a game, not an ad.

## Hook bank (first words / on-screen text)

- "This car costs ___ in Korea. Guess how much in [city]."
- "Why are Koreans selling cars this cheap?"
- "Don't buy a car from Korea until you watch this."
- "I just bought a car for a stranger in [country]."
- "Korean dealers don't want you to see this."
- "$20,000. Korea vs [country]. Same car."
- "This is where 1,000 cars leave Korea every day."
- "Tell me your budget, I'll show you what Korea gives you."
- "Korean grandpa drove this car 12,000 km in 4 years."
- "POV: your car is leaving Korea."

## Psychological triggers to stack

| Trigger | How to show it |
|---|---|
| Greed / saving | Price comparison, "you overpay $7,000 at home" |
| Curiosity | Hidden prices, "guess", reveal at the end |
| Trust | Real documents, real port, real clients receiving cars |
| FOMO | "Prices rising next month", "only 1 left at this price" (only if true) |
| Status | Genesis, Palisade, Carnival as "boss cars" |
| Fear | "3 traps when importing", flood cars, odometer fraud |
| Belonging | Speaking the audience's language, naming their city |

## Platform notes

- **TikTok**: raw, fast, trend sounds, reply-to-comment videos, LIVE from auctions/port.
- **Instagram Reels**: slightly more polished, carousels for price breakdowns, Stories for DMs.
- **YouTube Shorts**: evergreen ("How to import a car from Korea in 2026"). Link to long videos.
- Post the same video on all three. Change only the caption.
- Post 1–2 videos a day. Volume wins early.

## Metrics that matter

- 3-second hold rate above 70% means the hook works.
- Average watch time above 60% of length means the story works.
- Shares and saves are what drive 10M+ views.
- Comments containing the CTA keyword show the video sells.
