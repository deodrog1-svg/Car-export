## Market facts

- Korea's used-car exports hit a **record of about $8.9B in 2025** (up about 75% year on year).
  Central Asia was the largest destination by value, and **SUVs and crossovers were about 69% of value**.
- Most used cars now leave **Incheon** in **containers** (about 80% in 2025) instead of RoRo ships,
  because car-carrier space is scarce and container rates have fallen.
- Major destination regions: Central Asia (Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan),
  the Caucasus (Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan), the Middle East (Jordan, UAE, Iraq, Libya),
  Mongolia, Africa, Latin America (Chile, Dominican Republic and others), and Russia (restricted, see below).

## Why Korean used cars sell (the selling points for videos)

1. **Low mileage and clean condition.** Koreans change cars often, and most drive in cities.
2. **Transparent history.** Korea has an official insurance accident history database
   (Carhistory / 카히스토리) and mandatory performance inspection reports (성능점검기록부).
   Show these on camera. They build huge trust.
3. **Rich options.** Korean-market trims are often loaded: ventilated/heated seats, HUD,
   panoramic roof, 360° cameras.
4. **Price.** Korean cars depreciate fast at home, so a 3–5 year old car is often far cheaper
   than the same car at the destination.
5. **Left-hand drive.** This fits most of the world except UK/Japan-style markets.

## Imported cars in Korea (German cars are a major export category)

Korea is one of the biggest import-car markets in Asia. About 280,000 new imported cars were sold in Jan–Sep 2026
(Tesla #1, then BMW about 58k and Mercedes-Benz about 43k). After 3–5 years they flood the used market.
([Just Auto](https://www.just-auto.com/newsletters/south-korean-import-sales-rise-6-in-september))

**Why used German cars from Korea sell:**
- **Fast depreciation:** many Koreans sell German cars when the warranty ends (fear of repair costs), so prices drop hard.
- **Official dealer service history:** most were serviced at official BMW/Mercedes centres. Show the service printout on camera.
- **Low mileage, city use, rich options**, and the same Carhistory accident check as Korean cars.

**Popular German models on the Korean used market:** BMW 5 Series (520i/520d/530i), BMW 3 Series, X3, X5;
Mercedes-Benz E-Class (E250/E300/E220d), C-Class, GLC, GLE; Audi A6, A4, Q5; VW Tiguan, Passat; Porsche Cayenne/Macan; MINI.
Also Volvo, Lexus, Toyota and Tesla.

**Market fit:**
- **Russia: limited.** Korea requires an export permit for engines **over 2,000cc** and bans cars valued over **$50,000**
  ([Gulf Business 2026](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/automotive/south-korea-cracks-down-on-illegal-car-exports-to-russia/),
  [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/newsView/ked202402200011)), and most German 2.0 turbo engines are **over 160 hp**,
  which means an expensive recycling fee. Only low-power, at most 2.0 L, under-$50k versions can work. Check the exact hp of the specific car.
- **Central Asia, Caucasus, Mongolia, Middle East: strong.** There's no 160 hp penalty, and German status cars at Korean prices are a huge price gap.

## Restrictions to always check

- **Russia**: Korea requires an export permit for cars with engines **over 2,000cc** (since 24.02.2024) and bans cars
  valued **over $50,000**. Customs is actively prosecuting false declarations via third countries (2026). Russia's recycling fee
  (утильсбор) raises costs sharply. Re-exports through third countries carry legal risk, so don't promote them.
- **Russia 2026: recycling fee reform (01.01.2026).** Only cars **up to 160 hp** remain profitable
  (e.g. Sorento 2.2D 197 hp to Moscow ≈ 5.27M ₽ vs 3.2–3.8M ₽ market). See `market-intel/insights.md`.
- **Kyrgyzstan: new EAEU valuation formula from 01.04.2026.** Under-valuation "via Bishkek" no longer works.
- **Kyrgyzstan EVs (29.09.2026):** the 2026 duty-free EV quota (25,000 units) is exhausted; EVs now pay 15% duty
  (economist.kg). Don't promise duty-free EVs to Bishkek.
- **Kazakhstan personal import (from 22/28.09.2026):** individuals import only cars up to 5 years old, Euro-5+; recycling
  fee must be confirmed before release; SBKTS for personal import from third countries at most once per calendar year
  (bank.kz, Gov. decree No. 808 of 10.09.2026) **[VERIFY details]**.
- **Georgia hybrids:** importer listings show ≈60% reduced excise for 1.6 HEV (960 ₾ vs 2,397 ₾) **[VERIFY]**.
- **EAEU countries** (Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Belarus, Russia) need vehicle certification
  (SBKTS / electronic passport EPTS) **[VERIFY]**.
- Some African and Middle East countries require **pre-shipment inspection** and have **age limits** **[VERIFY]**.

## Documents (Korea side to client)

1. **Vehicle registration certificate** (자동차등록증) from the seller
2. **Export deregistration certificate** (수출말소사실증명서). The car is deregistered for export.
3. **Export declaration certificate** (수출신고필증) from Korea Customs
4. **Commercial invoice** and **packing list**
5. **Bill of Lading (B/L)** from the shipping line or forwarder
6. **Insurance certificate** (if CIF)
7. **Performance inspection report** and **Carhistory accident report** (for trust, show them in videos)
8. Destination-specific: certificate of origin, pre-shipment inspection, translation, certification **[VERIFY]**

## Process (great for a "journey" video series)

Find car (dealer / auction such as Lotte, Hyundai Glovis, K Car, or Encar listings) → inspect and film →
client pays deposit → buy and deregister → inland truck to Incheon → export customs → load container
(4 cars with racks in a 40ft) or RoRo → sail (2–6 weeks depending on route) → destination port →
customs clearance → certification and registration → delivery to client city.
